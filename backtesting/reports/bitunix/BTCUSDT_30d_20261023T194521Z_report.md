Overall confidence is aligned selected timeframes divided by the selected timeframe count; the requested >=80% is applied to that ratio. In a single-timeframe run, this does not validate multi-timeframe confluence. This is an explicit interpretation, not an exchange-defined confidence score.
- Decisions use closed bars only; a 15m signal enters at the next 15m open. One position at a time; position sizing uses 1x notional up to the specified equity fraction. Reverse qualified signal exits/reverses; otherwise position stays open.
- Historical funding rates are applied at settlement timestamps using reported mark price; positive funding is paid by longs and received by shorts. Trading fees are modeled as 0 bps and slippage as 0 bps because the account fee tier and fill data were not provided. Results are therefore pre-fee and idealized. No leverage above 1x, liquidation, spread, partial fills, stop-loss/TP, or exchange contract precision is simulated.
